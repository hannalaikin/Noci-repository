Your questions

Frequently asked questions

Anything else, text your stylist.

What is in the bag?

A complimentary personal stylist. She reads your proportions, coloring and closet, builds looks for what's on your calendar, talks them through with you, and sources the pieces.

Who is this for?

Any woman who wants to look put together without buying more.

Can't I just ask a chatbot?

A chatbot can't measure your rise from a photo or see your closet. Its advice is the same for everyone. Ours starts from your photos.

What photos do I need?

Front, side, and you in the outfit you feel best in.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. Your stylist pulls from your closet, from our made-to-order line, and from the brands you already shop. She'll tell you which is worth it.

Why is it complimentary?

It's how we introduce ourselves. Some clients go on to order from our made-to-order line, some don't. Either way you leave with a stylist and a guide.

What happens to my photos?

Seen only by your stylist. Never used in marketing without your written permission.

Will this make me feel worse about my body?

It's a map of how clothes sit on your body, not a critique of it. Most of the dread comes from guessing, and a read replaces the guessing with a reason.

  • You learn what's working. Features you dress around are often your strongest lines.
  • You learn what to change. Usually a hem or a rise, not your body.
  • You gain control. Know your line and your contrast, and you can make the call without us.
